# Phases

`plan.py` sets the dates. The sale always ends Cyber Monday night; `sale_length` sets when it opens.

| sale_length | Sale opens | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| `1w` | Tuesday before Black Friday | most stores; short, strong urgency |
| `2w` | two Tuesdays before | stores with a big list and slower-deciding buyers |
| `1m` | about Nov 1 to 4 | "all November" pricing; needs fresh creative every week to avoid fatigue |

## What each phase is for

- **Build (up to 7 days).** Audit, data, offers, tools, sample, creative set, staging, phone walk, owner review. With fewer days the planner squeezes these into the days available. Nothing in the list is optional; the review just gets shorter.
- **Warm-up (whatever is left before the teaser).** Two jobs: grow the list that gets early access, and find winning ad concepts cheaply on the regular offer. No sale prices anywhere. One value email a week.
- **Teaser (up to 7 days before opening).** The date is public. Prices stay hidden unless the owner chooses otherwise. The day before: early-access list is told the offers.
- **Sale.** Open to the early-access list first, the full list a few hours later. New angle every third day. On `2w` and `1m`, add concepts weekly and pause ads over the cap.
- **Peak (Thanksgiving to Sunday).** Highest budget. Thanksgiving is one light email. Black Friday gets a morning send and an evening resend to non-openers. Check spend and cost per new customer twice a day.
- **Cyber Monday.** Morning send and "ends tonight". Close at the stated time.
- **Close (the week after).** Verify everything ended, thank customers, report.

## Short on time

Cut in this order: warm-up, then teaser, then the number of creative concepts (never below three: price offer, ladder, one real review). Never cut: the audit, the cart test, the owner's approval, the "no early promotion" check.

## Installed after the sale should have opened

The planner opens the sale two days out. Build the three core concepts, one email per remaining peak day and one text for the last night.

## Before each phase starts

1. Does the live site show only what this phase allows?
2. Are discounts scheduled for the right hour in the store's time zone?
3. Is anything from last year (drafts, codes, old ads) able to send or apply by accident?
4. Is stock enough for the offers being promoted?
